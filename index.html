    📘 San José recycling rules

    🔵 Recycling: clean paper and cardboard, cans, glass bottles, and accepted rigid plastic containers. Empty containers and place items loose.

    ⚫ Garbage: used napkins, disposable coffee cups, wrappers, flexible plastic bags, and food scraps.

    🟠 Special handling: batteries, electronics, paint, and hazardous products. Neither bin is appropriate.

    A photo cannot always identify plastic types, whether a container is empty, or whether it contains hazardous substances. When unsure, check the label and official rules.
